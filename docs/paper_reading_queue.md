## Reading Template
For each paper, document:
1. What problem does it solve?
2. What are the inputs?
3. What are the outputs?
4. Who are the baselines?
5. What metrics are used?
6. Where is the data?
7. Can the code run?
8. Can I reproduce one figure in 1 week?
